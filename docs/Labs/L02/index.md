# Lab 2

Explain one design rule or guideline for additive manufacturing, and why it matters.

Design angled feature at 45 degrees or higher when possible as features at this angle require no support and thus less materials, while features below that angle normally require support material. Following this rule can save time, materials, and lower requirements.


FDM specific consideration, what it is and how a designer works around it.

Layer adhesion is a concern in FDM because the bond between the printed layers can be weaker than the material, making parts more likely to split under certain loads and forces. A designer can work around this by orienting the part so the primary loads are carried along stronger layer direction and by using appropriate printing temperatures and extrusion settings to improve bonding between layers.
